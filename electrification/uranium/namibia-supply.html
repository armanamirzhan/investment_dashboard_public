Namibia supply gap

Chinese equity mines are a pillar of China’s imports — but cannot cover the full +9–10 ktU increment alone.

As of Sep 30, 2026
← Uranium hub Not investment advice

Direct answer

Correct — Namibia cannot cover China’s full +~9–10 ktU/yr increment alone. Country output was about ~8.5–9 ktU in 2025; Chinese-linked share roughly ~6.5–7.5 ktU. Realistic incremental Namibia by ~2030 is on the order of +2–5 ktU — well short of +9–10, and not all Namibian pounds are China-only.

Current Chinese-linked assets

  • Husab (CGN via Taurus / Swakop, ~90% effective) — ~5.23 kt U₃O₈ (~4.4 ktU) in 2025; design ~6 kt U₃O₈ ≈ 5.1 ktU.
  • Rössing (CNNC / CNOL ~68.62%) — ~3.19 kt U₃O₈ (~2.7 ktU) in 2025.
  • Langer Heinrich (CNNC / CNOL 25%; Paladin majority) — ~1.55–1.85 ktU class; only minority China equity claim.

Together, Chinese-controlled or linked tonnes already run ~6.5–7.5 ktU — China’s second pillar beside Kazakhstan. That output is already largely spoken for; it is not spare capacity sitting idle for the increment.

Pipeline upside (still not enough)

  • Husab ramp toward / above ~5.1 ktU design — modest incremental vs current ~4.4.
  • Etango (Bannerman / CNOL development & offtake) — ~1.3 ktU/yr if built.
  • Tumas — ~1.4 ktU/yr if built.
  • Optimistic country total by 2030 maybe ~11–13 ktU — still leaves Namibia unable to absorb +9–10 ktU alone, even before noting that not every Namibian pound is China-destined.

Typical China-accessible increment framing: +2–4 ktU; ≤~5 ktU optimistic.

Ceiling check

Even if all of Namibia’s current ~8.5–9 ktU went to China (it doesn’t), that volume is already the equity/import pillar — not incremental headroom equal to China’s reactor-driven +9–10 ktU. Closing the gap still requires Kazakhstan (corporate LTCs + equity JVs), plus domestic China mines, other origins, and/or stockpile timing. See Kazakhstan / KAP and China U demand.
