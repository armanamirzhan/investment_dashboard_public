Thesis

ABB’s Infinitus portfolio targets source-to-rack 800 V DC for AI data centers: SST-based conversion, DC distribution, and solid-state DC protection. The SACE Infinitus solid-state circuit breaker (IEC 60947-2 class messaging; high amp / ~1.25 kV DC class) matters because DC fault clearing is harder than AC. Components guided toward production within ~12 months; full DC-native halls more like 2–3 years.
