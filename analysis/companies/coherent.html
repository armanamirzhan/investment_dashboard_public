Thesis

Coherent sells merchant SiC epitaxial wafers — including 200 mm — so device makers can ramp without owning crystal growth. Thick epi aimed at multi-kilovolt AI-datacenter and industrial power is a capacity differentiator, but lasers/networking still dilute how “pure” the equity is to SiC wafers.
