Thesis

Siemens Energy is the listed energy/grid company: turbines, large transformers, and transmission gear that AI campuses still need. Separately, Siemens AG with Maschinenfabrik Reinhausen announced a modular medium-voltage SST concept converting up to 36 kV AC to stable 800 V DC for AI halls — still in industrialization, not a catalog ship date. Treat SMNEY as grid/turbine exposure with group SST optionality, not a pure 800 VDC pure-play.
