Thesis

Vertiv builds the power gear next to the graphics processing unit (GPU) rack — sidecars, busways, and backup — so operators can feed 800 V DC into dense AI racks without ripping out the whole facility at once. Its PowerDirect 5000-class sidecar path and NVIDIA collaboration put it on the near-term rack transition (optional Vera Rubin H2 2026; volume ~Rubin Ultra H2 2027), while facility-wide DC halls remain a later (~2029+) chapter.
