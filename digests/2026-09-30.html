AI Infra Digest — 2026-09-30

Baseline reference: /workspace/ai-infra-baseline/BASELINE.md (as of 2026-09-27). Summary (5 bullets max)

  • Power / MV (medium voltage): At YOTTA 2026 (Sep 28), Schneider Electric unveiled what it calls the first fully software-defined MV switchgear, in live pilot with Equinix; claims up to 3× faster ordering/manufacturing and 2× faster commissioning vs conventional engineered-to-order MV. Also launched RM AirSeT 38 SF₆ (sulfur hexafluoride)-free GIS (gas-insulated switchgear) to 38 kV — order intake planned 2027, first deliveries 2028. Separately (Sep 29), Eaton + Infineon partnered on SiC (silicon carbide) devices for Eaton’s MVSST 2.0 solid-state transformer targeting 800 VDC AI data-center architectures (APAC platform 1.25–2.5 MW, 10 kV AC → 800 VDC).
  • HBM (high bandwidth memory) pricing (secondary): TrendForce (circulated Sep 29–30) forecasts mixed HBM ASP (average selling price) +121% in 2027 vs 2026 on shortage + HBM4/HBM4E mix; does not mean every SKU doubles. Tightness into 2027 reinforced; wait for Micron FY26 Q4 AMC today for primary company print.
  • Fiber: AT&T and Corning (GLW) signed a multi-year fiber-and-cable supply agreement valued at >$3B (Sep 29) to support AT&T’s path to 60M fiber locations by 2030 — primarily telecom FTTH (fiber-to-the-home), with AI/data demand cited as a usage driver; not a CoWoS (chip on wafer on substrate) or InP (indium phosphide; see photonics) capacity event.
  • Optics tape / analyst: Optics complex sold off Sep 28 with the AI-hardware group, then rebounded Sep 29 (LITE ~+7%, COHR/GLW ~+5% intraday prints in trade press) on Citi optical-circuit-switching TAM (~$11B by 2030) and a GF Securities note arguing Nvidia CPO (co-packaged optics; see photonics) deployment is accelerating. Incremental PhotonLink staging (trade press Sep 29): CPO scale-out Q4 CY2026, CPO scale-up + NPO (near-packaged optics; see photonics) 2H 2027, chip-to-chip ~2029–2030; Nvidia named as public CPO LTA (long-term agreement) customer.
  • Calendar: Micron FY26 Q4 still due Wed Sep 30 after the close (call 2:30 p.m. MT / 4:30 p.m. ET) — not yet reported as of this morning-ET digest. Next primary HBM refresh trigger.
